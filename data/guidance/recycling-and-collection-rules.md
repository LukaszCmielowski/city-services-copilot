# Recycling and collection rules

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/trash-recycling-city-upkeep/

Before reporting a missed collection, verify the scheduled collection day and that the materials were prepared according to current city guidance. Trash and recycling are separate collection streams, and an issue with one does not necessarily apply to the other.

Collection can be affected by holidays, weather, blocked access, improperly prepared materials, or operational conditions. A detailed request should identify whether the issue concerns trash or recycling and include the scheduled date.

For oversized material, hazardous waste, or other special items, use the service intended for that material instead of a missed-collection report.
