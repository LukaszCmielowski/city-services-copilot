# Report a pothole

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/streets-sidewalks-alleys/report-a-pothole/

Use 311 to report a pothole on a city street. Include the closest address or intersection, travel direction when relevant, and a description of the road condition. A report helps the Streets Department identify the location and plan an inspection or repair.

Requests are prioritized by the condition reported, traffic and safety considerations, and available crews. The final service-request status can be followed through 311. Do not enter traffic or stand in an unsafe location to collect information for a report.
