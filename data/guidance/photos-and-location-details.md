# Provide useful location details and photos

Source: https://www.phila.gov/311/

Accurate locations help city teams find a reported condition. When an exact street address is unavailable, use the nearest intersection, block number, landmark, travel direction, or other visible reference. For equipment such as a streetlight, a pole number can be helpful when visible from a safe location.

Photos can provide context for conditions such as graffiti, dumping, pavement damage, or damaged signs. Do not take a photo from an unsafe position, enter private property, or interact with hazards to create a report.

Include only information needed to route and understand the issue. Do not add unnecessary personal information about other people to a public-service request.
