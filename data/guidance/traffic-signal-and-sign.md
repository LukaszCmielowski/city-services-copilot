# Report a traffic signal or sign problem

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/streets-sidewalks-alleys/report-a-problem-on-a-city-street-sidewalk-or-alley/

Use 311 to report a malfunctioning traffic light, damaged traffic sign, missing sign, or other traffic-control concern. Provide the intersection, direction of travel, the signal or sign involved, and a concise description of the condition.

Traffic-control problems are assessed for safety and routed to the appropriate maintenance team. Do not attempt to direct traffic or repair city equipment. Use 911 for an immediate collision risk or emergency traffic hazard.
