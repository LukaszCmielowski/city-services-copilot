# Report a missing or damaged manhole cover

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/streets-sidewalks-alleys/report-a-problem-on-a-city-street-sidewalk-or-alley/

Use 311 to report a missing, loose, sunken, or damaged manhole cover. Give the closest address or intersection and describe whether the issue is in a travel lane, bike lane, sidewalk, or another location.

The report is routed for inspection and to identify the responsible utility or city team. Keep a safe distance from the opening or damaged cover. Call 911 for an immediate danger to road users or pedestrians.
