# Report a sanitation code violation

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/trash-recycling-city-upkeep/report-a-problem-with-trash-recycling-or-city-upkeep/

Use 311 to report a sanitation condition that may violate city requirements, such as recurring unmanaged waste at a property. Provide the location, the observed condition, and when it was observed. Do not include unnecessary personal information about residents or neighbors.

The City can review the report and determine whether inspection or enforcement is appropriate. The service request tracks the report; enforcement actions and timing depend on the facts found during review.
