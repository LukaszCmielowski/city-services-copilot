# Report a property, building, or lot condition

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/property-lots-housing/report-a-problem-with-a-building-lot-or-street/

311 can receive reports about building, lot, and property conditions, including maintenance problems, suspected code issues, vacant-building concerns, and conditions affecting the public way. Describe the visible condition and location without adding unnecessary personal information about occupants.

The report can be routed for review or inspection. An inspection may determine whether a condition falls within city enforcement authority and whether follow-up is needed. A service request is not proof of a violation and does not replace emergency reporting.

Call 911 for an immediate danger, such as a condition that creates an urgent threat to people or property.
