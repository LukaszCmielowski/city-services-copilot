# Philadelphia 311 service overview

Source: https://www.phila.gov/311/

Philadelphia 311 accepts non-emergency service requests through its website, mobile application, phone service, and other city channels. A good request identifies the location, describes the issue, and includes supporting photos when it is safe to do so.

311 is not an emergency-response service. Call 911 when there is an immediate danger, fire, crime in progress, medical emergency, or another urgent threat to life or property. Service-request status can change from received to in progress, completed, or closed with an explanation.
