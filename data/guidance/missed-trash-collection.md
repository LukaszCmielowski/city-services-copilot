# Report missed trash collection

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/trash-recycling-city-upkeep/report-a-missed-trash-or-recycling-pickup/

Use 311 to report household trash or recycling that was not collected on its scheduled day. Give the address, collection type, and the date it was missed. Keep containers accessible according to city collection guidance.

The request is routed to sanitation operations for review. Collection can depend on the service schedule, weather, access, holidays, and operational conditions. Check the 311 request for updates instead of submitting duplicate reports for the same pickup.
