# Schedule collection for bulky household items

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/trash-recycling-city-upkeep/

Large household items may require a scheduled collection rather than a standard weekly trash pickup. Residents should review the city’s current instructions before placing appliances, tires, furniture, or other oversized items at the curb.

The service route can depend on the item type and applicable preparation rules. Some items require an appointment, special handling, or a different disposal option. Hazardous materials and items containing regulated substances should not be placed out for regular collection.

When a bulky-item request is appropriate, include the address, item type, and any requested scheduling information. Follow the instructions provided with the request before setting the item out.
