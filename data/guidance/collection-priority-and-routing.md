# Service priority and routing

Source: https://www.phila.gov/311/

The 311 system records and routes non-emergency service needs. Priority can depend on safety, the service category, the location, condition severity, access, legal responsibility, weather, and crew availability.

Two reports of similar issues may not receive the same timing because they can require different inspections, equipment, permits, utility coordination, or enforcement steps. A predicted resolution time is an operational estimate, not a service promise.

Clear category selection and location details improve routing. Residents should use emergency channels when a condition requires immediate intervention rather than waiting for a routine service-request workflow.
