# Report an abandoned vehicle

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/streets-sidewalks-alleys/report-a-problem-on-a-city-street-sidewalk-or-alley/

Use 311 to report a vehicle that appears abandoned on a public street. Describe the location, vehicle type, color, license plate if safely visible, and the reason it appears abandoned. Do not confront occupants or enter a vehicle.

The report is reviewed by the responsible city team. A vehicle may need inspection and notice before enforcement or removal, so a report does not guarantee immediate towing. Use emergency channels for an immediate safety hazard.
