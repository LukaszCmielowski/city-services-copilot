# Emergency and non-emergency routing

Source: https://www.phila.gov/311/

311 is for non-emergency information and service requests. It is appropriate for reporting routine city-service conditions and tracking the resulting request.

Call 911 for an immediate emergency or urgent threat, including a condition that presents immediate danger to people, traffic, or property. Examples can include a fire, crime in progress, medical emergency, immediate electrical danger, or an urgent roadway hazard.

When in doubt about immediate safety, use emergency channels rather than waiting for a routine 311 workflow. The correct routing choice helps the city respond through the appropriate system.
