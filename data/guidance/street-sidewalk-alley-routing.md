# Street, sidewalk, and alley issue routing

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/streets-sidewalks-alleys/report-a-problem-on-a-city-street-sidewalk-or-alley/

311 helps route reports involving city streets, sidewalks, alleys, potholes, graffiti, streetlights, traffic controls, and damaged utility covers. Pick the most specific service type available so the request reaches the team best suited to review it.

Location is especially important for street-related reports. Provide an address, intersection, or clear landmark, and state whether the condition is in a roadway, sidewalk, alley, bike lane, or another part of the public right-of-way.

Reports that describe an immediate hazard require emergency response rather than routine service routing. Do not enter traffic or approach damaged equipment to gather details.
