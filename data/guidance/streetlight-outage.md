# Report a streetlight outage

Source: https://www.phila.gov/services/streets-sidewalks-alleys/report-a-streetlight-outage/

Use 311 to report a streetlight that is off, flickering, damaged, or operating during daylight. Include the nearest address or intersection and, if visible from a safe location, the pole number or other location detail.

The request is reviewed and routed to the responsible maintenance team. Repair timing can depend on the reported condition, electrical access, safety needs, and required equipment. Do not touch damaged electrical equipment or wires; report an immediate electrical hazard through emergency channels.
